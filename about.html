About me

The Fullstack Developer App is an online tool that helps you export ready-made fullstack templates ready to work as your future website or web application. It helps you combine front-end slides, panels, and UI components with back-end routes, APIs, authentication, and database models, and export everything as a complete set of source files: HTML/CSS/JS, Node.js/Express, and SQL/NoSQL. As a fullstack developer, you work across the entire stack—designing the user interface, writing client-side logic, building server-side services, managing databases, and deploying the final product.
